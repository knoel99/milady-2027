The defects are about the **status of results** (measured, published or conjectured), **traceability** (numbers missing from the digest, references missing from `docs/specs/sources/`) and **progression**.

## 2. Recurring defects (fix the same way everywhere)

1. **E1 status.** "Merger crisis", "chaos", "jump of the law", "no reversal" are stated without measurement (P3, P4, report, thesis). Chaos is published only at $\Gamma = 0.9$. "No reversal for $\Gamma \le 0.86$" means none observed over the simulated time.
2. **Numbers missing from the digest**: Rice error percentages (4, 6, 14, 17, 23, 32, 42, 65 %), 0.77 and 0.82 (thesis), 0.7 % of time with $D < 0$, 17 %, the asymmetry floor 0.004. Extend `experiments/digest.py` to produce them with their definition, window, floor and seed count.
3. **Seed counts not stated** in summaries: E1 has 2, E2 has 1, the Rice grid uses seed 0 of model C only. The "halved" gain sits exactly at the report's own significance threshold.
4. **E2 variant names.** P8's "projected flow" and Prop. 2 (b) are not E2's V3, which is a "flow in $S$" trained with projected noise; V3's $2\cdot10^{-8}$ is single-precision round-off; V3 is the best variant on test $\eta$, on one seed. "Five mechanisms" counts V0, the free baseline.
5. **Exponential tilting called "exact" or "gentle"** (P8, report, thesis). Exact only for the imposed moments, on the tuning half, while the ESS holds; in E2 the ESS drops to one sample in 500 at three of five test $\eta$. It does improve the law where the ESS holds ("improved no law" is false). Resampling with duplicates under a V-statistic biases the energy distance against tilting.
6. **Energy distance.** Three estimators coexist (V-statistic in P5 and `common.py`, which returns $D^2$; U-statistic in P5 §5.6); $\sqrt{\mathcal E}$ is the distance; normalisations differ (MMD² = ℰ in P5, ℰ = 2 MMD² in P7 and `data-metrics.md`). Proposal: one definition in P5 with both estimators and their floor, ℰ = 2 MMD², report the excess over the floor.
7. **Over-favourable readings of E2.** "V5, the only one to improve the law" (P5, report Fig. 10 caption) is false: per-sample rescaling also improves the energy distance but crushes $\sigma(D)$. Interpolation in $\eta$ is called "correct" (report) and even extrapolation (P6). V5's gain is concentrated at large $\eta$. "Unbiased, does not crush the variance" is contradicted by E1 and by the training $\eta$.
8. **Jensen gap vs. constraint in law**: P7 conflates the two arguments; the report separates them correctly.
9. **Traceability.** Dozens of references are missing from `docs/specs/sources/`: Rincon 2019, Faranda 2014 (P3); Chandler & Kerswell, Avila, Mukund & Hof, Orszag (P2); classical references of P6 and P7, EDM settings; Köhler–Klein–Noé, Cohen–Welling (P8); Baldan 2026 (thesis). Csiszár 1975 rests on an `[UNCONFIRMED]` entry. Novelty claims rest on an unconfirmed literature watch; check transition-state theory (Bennett–Chandler) as prior art for the Rice claim.
10. **Notation clashes**: $\eta$ (Kolmogorov scale in P2, magnetic diffusivity elsewhere → use $\ell_K$), $\lambda$ (control parameter, growth rate → $\gamma$, loss weight), and $\beta$, $\mu$, $\nu$, $\Gamma$, $\mathcal E$, $V$.

## 4. Thesis protocol (to tighten before any claim)

- Define the energy distance precisely; weight instead of resampling; floor between two independent sets, block bootstrap for correlated series.
- Train / validation (for $\beta$ and other settings) / inner test / outer test sets; memorisation test at stage 3.
- At least five seeds for any comparison with a criterion; report min and max; recompute the budget (the $128^3$ model already contradicts H1–H2).
- Replace non-discriminating criteria: asymmetry (true by construction), factor 2 (already reached within noise), criterion on the optimised identity, $10^{-12}$ (out of reach in single precision), Poisson interval (unfalsifiable).
- Stage 3: compute $\dot D$ at full resolution (sub-filter EMF missing on filtered fields); treat periodic LATU data with smoothed $\mu_r$ separately; minimum number of reversals and of in-reversal snapshots; unified $Re$ and $Rm$ conventions; dynamo threshold from control parameters.
- Drop the Kazantsev slope in the linear phase (not stationary); stage 4 (DRESDYN) is partly a regression; Prop. 2.41 (symmetric does not mean bimodal).

## 5. Didactic progression (frozen)

The course assumes L2 tools from P2 on (linear ODEs, change of variables, surface and volume integrals, diagonalisation and SVD), never teaches local electromagnetism or spherical coordinates, uses notions before their module, proves several results twice (Leray, potentials, flow matching, Fokker–Planck, I-projection, stationarity identities, Gissinger, DPS) and announces 215 h for an estimated 330–360 h. Kept as a record; not on the critical path.

Found on the way, now fixed, with consequences for the published E1 numbers:

- **The first E1 model of each process was not reproducible.** It was built before any seeding, and torch 2.14 starts from a random seed (checked: two fresh processes give different `initial_seed()`). In a forward run without cache this is `A_reference` seed 0, which then propagates to `A_reference_half` and `D_tilting_A` (the old and new code differ only there in `--quick`); with `--reverse` or a partial cache, it is the first model that process trains (`C_court` of the last seed with `--reverse`). How the published run was launched is not recorded, so which published values are affected cannot be told. `e1_gissinger.py` now seeds before building each model, so a re-run of E1 will differ from the published values for every variant. The five-seed re-run replaces them.
- **E2 summary**: the absolute value of the balance residuals was lost in an earlier rename (`startswith("residu")`); fixed before any re-run. Published values were computed with it.
