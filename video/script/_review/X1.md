## Remaining doubts (not changed)

- **X1.2 has no data yet.** `data/X1_modes3d.json` does not exist, and `tools/data_existing.py` has no `x1_modes3d` converter. X1.2 has no pending variant, so if it is rendered as a pending seat, its narration describes shapes that are not on screen. The file must exist before render; otherwise the lead should hold X1.2 back.
- **Phase of oscillating modes.** Bouya & Dormy put a complex dominant eigenvalue in the first window. A stored snapshot cannot tell whether "Rm 12 spreads out" is a property of the mode or of the phase at t = 150. The narration hedges ("in these snapshots"). The lead may prefer the report-only fallback given in `notes`.
- **Mislabelled series.** `tools/data_existing.py:353` still names series[0] "whole run". It is the second-half fit (R:1066, e3_abc.py:85). This is the writer's request to the foundation engineer.
- **Above the bound.** −0.50005 sits a hair below the bound −0.5 (by about the gap between the two fits). The narration says "on the bound", and the magnifier and chip show the gap. A pedantic viewer may still notice it.
- **Who to ask.** `ask_who` "Y. Ponty / C. Nore" was kept. Y. Ponty is the ABC reference (T:47, T:908). C. Nore was kept for kinematic growth-rate computations in SFEMaNS; the lead may narrow it to Y. Ponty, as in ch10.Q.
