## Changes

1. **11.1.s3 and 11.1.s4** (main narration and result variant). "…measured on an ordinary processor, with one or two training seeds." / "Toy problems, and machines tested only…" became "…measured on an ordinary processor." / "Toy problems, one or two training seeds, and machines tested only between the settings they were trained on."
   - E3 is a kinematic eigenvalue solver, with nothing trained. Saying that three experiments measured "with one or two training seeds" put E3 under a seed count it does not have.
   - The seeds now sit in the caveat sentence, which is about the machines (R:745, R:1179).
   - The change also saves one word.
2. **11.1, DRESDYN bet** (params.bets and its on-screen label). "{param}Rm{/}_c within 10 % and right symmetry at an unseen Po" became "{param}Rm{/} threshold within 10 % and right symmetry class at an unseen {param}Po{/}".
   - `Kit.markup` has no subscript rule (runtime/components/kit.js:105-110), so "_c" would show as a literal underscore on screen.
   - T:952 says "correct symmetry class", not just "symmetry".
3. **11.1 result variant, `data_fields`.** The list was [n_in, n_bets]. It now holds all nine ledger fields that the sentences and their `when` conditions read.
   - `cond_ok` treats a missing boolean as false (assemble_cut.py:170-176).
   - So a ledger that forgot `top_awaiting` or `any_not_met` would silently drop a sentence: "the top two steps still await…", or worse, the count of missed rules.
   - With the full list, the variant waits until the ledger contract is met.
4. **11.1 sources.** The T:903 entry said "tier 1 criteria", as if the tier-1 row came from the plan. It does not.
   - The tier-1 row is the 5 % data check of `tools/data_cloud.py` conv_palier1. ch10's 10.3 says this explicitly: it is not a criterion of the PhD plan.
   - The source entry now says so.
5. **11.2.s3** "For the Earth, no one can say yet." became "For the Earth, no one can say."
   - NOAA says "we cannot state for certain if or when" and implies no future predictability. The word "yet" added an unsourced claim (critique B2).
   - Matching fix on screen: the chip 'for Earth: no one can say when yet · NOAA NCEI' became 'for Earth: no one can say if or when · NOAA NCEI'.
   - The visual already promised "the ch00 chip, verbatim", and the old text was not verbatim.
6. **11.2.s4** "In a simple computer model, chaos erases the memory of the present too fast." became "In a simple computer model, chaos makes the forecast fade before the average wait ends."
   - "Too fast" had no reference point, and "the memory of the present" is hard to parse by ear.
   - The new line names the actual comparison (P4:1627: the error grows past the mean interval between crossings). It is a near-verbatim callback to 04.4 ("the forecast fades before the average wait ends"), a motif payoff.
   - It also avoids "long before", which the ch04 notes rule out: the horizon is about 150 against a mean wait of about 226, and about half of the waits are shorter than the horizon.
7. **11.2.s5** "But it does answer {metric}how often{/} it flips, counted as crossings of zero." became "But {metric}how often{/} the field flips, counted as crossings of zero, can still be answered."
   - In the old sentence the two pronouns had no clear antecedent: the first "it" could be read as chaos or the model, the second as the model or the needle. The model "answering" was also odd.
   - The new wording echoes 00.4 ("Which one can we answer? … The second is not [hopeless]"), and "answered" chains into s6, "The answer hides in a shoebox".
8. **11.2.s10** "…roughly right far from the tipping point, and poor right at it." became "…roughly right far from the threshold, and poor near it."
   - "Tipping point" is used nowhere else in the film. The chip on screen during this sentence says "far from the threshold · 42 % near it", and ch09.6 says "roughly right far from the threshold, poor near it". A new word for a known concept, one minute from the end, invites "is that something else?".
   - The 42 % covers the window 0.865 ≤ Γ ≤ 0.885, which is "near" the threshold, not "right at it" (R:883).
   - Matching change in the visual: the word-timing anchor 'far from the tipping point' is now 'far from the threshold'.
9. **11.2.s11** "A smooth machine cannot follow a steep change." became "A smooth machine rounds off a steep change."
   - Proposition 9 is about a true jump. E1 has a steep ramp with one seed, and 09.3 says so carefully: "consistent with the theorem, not proof".
   - "Cannot follow" stated it as an impossibility. "Rounds off" describes what was measured: early leakage, then lag and overshoot (B17). It is also the stiff-sheet image of 09.3 ("the sheet rounds the corner").
   - The 11.2 sources now cite 09.3 and 04.4.
10. **Unit notes, question card.** The notes said that the brief asks for no question card on the closing. It does not: §3.1 says "a question card ends every chapter".
    - Keeping no card is a sound choice. ch10q, which plays just before, gathers and ranks every question. A card after the end card, or between the lesson and the end card, would break the closing.
    - The notes now record the choice as a deviation for the lead to confirm, instead of as a rule.
