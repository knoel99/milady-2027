## Changes

1. **02.3.s4: "That tiny ratio is called the magnetic Prandtl number, Pm" became "Their ratio, one in a hundred thousand, is the magnetic Prandtl number, Pm".**
   - The sentence before it says the field smudges about a hundred thousand times *faster*. "That tiny ratio" therefore pointed at 100 000, which is the inverse of Pm (Pm = ν/η = 8.2e-6, P3:197; R:137).
   - "One in a hundred thousand" fixes the direction and matches the on-screen `Pm = ν / η ≈ 1 / 100 000`.
   - To keep the word budget, s3 drops "out" and s5 becomes "Reaching an Rm of a few tens takes violent stirring."
   - The 02.3 label 'swirl smoothing' became 'swirl smudging', the word the narration uses (two occurrences: labels and visual).

2. **02.3.s7: "Every laboratory dynamo is fiercely turbulent" became "So every laboratory dynamo is turbulent".**
   - The source says "always turbulent" (P3:281) and "every laboratory dynamo is turbulent" (P3:1221). "Fiercely" overstates it for Riga and Karlsruhe, whose flows are constrained by pipes and cells (P3:1153: field fluctuations of about 0.2 %).
   - "So" makes the causal link to Re explicit.

3. **02.3 tank label: 'liquid sodium · about 120 °C' became 'liquid sodium'.** 120 °C is the VKS working temperature of Nore et al. 2018 (P3:169, P3:1161). Riga first ran at 205 °C (P3:1149). The generic tank must not suggest that every experiment runs at 120 °C. The temperature stays on the formula layer, and a source line for P3:1149 was added.

4. **02.4.s4: "And at Cadarache, VKS made a dipole that can flip" became "And only VKS, at Cadarache, made a dipole that can flip".**
   - Critique_accuracy B3 and the outline's key idea say only VKS flips (R:149: "first ... reversals in the laboratory, in VKS").
   - The old line left this to the visual alone. Riga's field "oscillates", which a listener can hear as flipping, so the narration now says it.

5. **02.4.s5: "is still trying, with a spinning cylinder" became "aims to join them: a spinning cylinder".** The sources say DRESDYN "seeks to achieve" a dynamo and is "under way" or "ongoing" (R:86; S:68; P3:1208, P3:1215). "Still trying" implied it had been running and failing, which the site does not say.

6. **02.4.s1: "each a different one" became "each a different kind".** "One" could be heard as "a different experiment".

7. **02.5.s2: "Two bladed discs at its ends spin in opposite directions, churning the sodium" became "At its ends, two bladed discs, the impellers, spin in opposite directions".** Later narration uses "impellers" without defining it: 03.5.s3 "What the impellers are made of: remember the iron", and 10.9. Until now the word appeared only in on-screen labels.

8. **02.5.s6: "So the threshold depends on more than speed" became "So the threshold depends on more than Rm: materials count too".**
   - With steel and with iron, the sodium and the speed are the same, so Rm is the same. The lesson is R:90 exactly: "depends on Rm but also on the materials".
   - The line now answers 02.2's "One number keeps the score" and sets up the knob panel of 03.5.
   - The knob label 'speed' became 'Rm', in both the labels and the visual note.

9. **02.6.s2: "The dipole starts to flip, again and again" became "At some settings, the dipole flips again and again".** The sources say the field *can* reverse when the impeller speeds differ (P3:1166: "can reverse irregularly"). Monchaux et al. 2009 report a family of regimes (P3:1166). Unequal speeds alone do not guarantee flips.

10. **02.6.s5: "The same uneven stripes as the seafloor" became "Uneven stripes, like the seafloor's, but written in minutes".**
    - The VKS strip is built from the qualitative normal-form curve, whose durations "are not those of VKS" (R:163). It has two sign changes (data/fig_vks.json: t = 68.6 and 235.2). "The same stripes" claimed a statistical likeness that nobody has measured.
    - The strip label 'VKS · minutes' became 'VKS, qualitative · minutes', in both the labels and the visual note.

11. **02.2.s2: added `{param}…{/}` on "magnetic diffusivity".** This matches 02.1.s5 and the green η of the hero equation, as the colour key requires.

12. **02.6 seafloor provenance.** `params.seafloor_source` and the Tauxe source line now name the table as the ch00 review verified it: "Composite GPTS of Gee & Kent (2007)", with Cande & Kent 1995 ages. The ages are identical to `ch00.json` 00.3.

13. **ch02.Q question card.**
    - Narration: "our first three-D runs" became "the first three-dimensional runs". Every other unit narrates "three dimensions", and "three-D" would show in the burned subtitles. "Our" is replaced by an impersonal phrasing, as BRIEF §6 asks.

## Checked and kept

- **Sodium numbers.**
  - ν = 6.78e-7 m²/s at 120 °C (P3:169).
  - Water 1e-6 m²/s (P2:1343).
  - Pm = 8.2e-6 = 0.82e-5 (P3:197; R:137).
  - Re = Rm/Pm ~ 1e6 to 1e7 for Rm ~ 10 to 50 (P3:281, P3:1145). The arithmetic checks: 10/8.2e-6 = 1.2e6 and 50/8.2e-6 = 6.1e6.
- **Rm.** Rm = UL/η = τ_η/τ_u (P3:259-263); Rm ≪ 1 versus Rm ≫ 1 (P3:269). The frozen-flux theorem holds for η = 0 only, which is why the line says "in a perfect conductor" (P3:305, P3:313).
- **Riga.** Helical flow; the field alternates and rotates slowly; first dynamo in November 1999 (P3:1149; R:139).
- **Karlsruhe.** 52 helical cells; steady, dipole-like field; first dynamo in December 1999 (P3:1153; R:139).
- **VKS.**
  - 150 L of sodium, up to 300 kW, 26 Hz top speed.
  - Steel gave no dynamo; soft iron gave a dynamo; first dynamo in 2006, published in 2007 (P3:1161-1165, P3:1214; R:139).
  - At 16 and 22 Hz: polarities of 120 s and 50 s, flips of about 5 s, ohmic time about 0.4 s (R:149; P3:1166).
- **Question card facts.**
  - Taylor–Green thresholds Rm_c = 30 at Pm = 1 and 226 at Pm = 0.3 (T:908).

## Remaining doubts

- **Lexicon.** `audio/lexicon.json` has no pins for Cadarache, Karlsruhe or Prandtl, which are all narrated. That file belongs to the audio engineer and was not touched. Suggested respellings: "kah-dah-RASH", "KARLS-roo-uh", "PRAHN-tl".
- **The letter F.** The 02.6 formula layer uses F₁ and F₂ for impeller frequencies, as the site and X6.0/X6.4 do. X2.5 and X6.6 use F for the drift of the dynamics. The symbol sheet does not cover F. This is for the consistency editor.
- **"≫" on the 02.6 formula layer.** It reads τ_rev ≈ 5 s ≫ τ_ohmic ≈ 0.4 s, where the site says "compared with" (a factor of 12). Kept.
- **Rm conventions on the card.** The "why" line quotes the thresholds of Ponty et al. 2005, which use their own Rm definition. The campaign uses another (U_ref, L_ref = 1/k_f; see ch10.5's formula layer). The card does not compare the two, so nothing is wrong, but the question could also ask about the convention.
- **"Laboratory dynamo" wording.** "Laboratory dynamos use liquid sodium" and "every laboratory dynamo is turbulent" follow P3:169 and P3:1221. They ignore solid-rotor lab dynamos, an external fact that is not on the site. Kept, because the chapter is about fluid dynamos.
- **Overlap between cards.** ch02.Q (which Pm values) and ch03.Q (spacing of the points) both touch the Re × Pm grid of the Taylor–Green runs. `script/_shared/` (the question register) does not exist yet.
