## Changes, with evidence

1. **10.4 bet: a criterion understated, then contradicted.**
   - The bet said "a long fit and a late fit agreeing on the sign". The criterion is stronger: |s − s_q| < |s| (`conv_abc3d`, D17, A2).
   - The next line said "those two fits already disagreed at eighteen". At 32³ and Rm 18 they agree on the sign: s = −9.07e-4 and s_q = −2.64e-3 (`data/e3_growth.json`). What fails is |s − s_q| > |s|.
   - New s4: "...with a long fit and a late fit that agree."
   - New s5: "At eighteen, the small sweep's two fits already differed by more than the rate itself."
   - The seat's `why` was updated to match.
2. **10.4.s2: "the small run" became "the small sweep".** E3 is 24 runs, Rm 2 to 30 (R:1066).
3. **10.5.s1: who pushes back.**
   - "counter-rotating eddies that now feel the field and push back" made the eddies push back. Physically, the Lorentz force is the field acting on the flow (CFG `palier2_tg3d.yaml`:3).
   - Split into s1 "Bet two, the first true dynamo: a Taylor-Green flow of counter-rotating eddies." and s2 "Now the field pushes back on the flow." (new cue `push`).
   - The visual's 'anchor+2.2 (on push back)' did not land on those words (about 8 s in). It is now `push-0.15`.
4. **10.5.s3: the seed grows only above threshold.** The line was "A tiny seed field should grow, then level off". It is false below threshold, and the bet itself is about a threshold (A8). It now reads "Above a threshold, a tiny seed should grow, then level off."
5. **10.5 confidence versus the D18 catch.**
   - Unsaturated runs do not weaken the bracketing bet: the threshold is read from the kinematic rate s_kin (`conv_tg3d`). They threaten the crowd laws.
   - New s6 gives the real reason for medium confidence: "Published runs define the Reynolds number differently, so the threshold may fall off this grid." Ponty et al. 2005 use R_V with u_rms and the integral scale. The campaign uses U_ref = 1 and L_ref = 0.5. At Pm = 2 the lowest Rm is 50.
   - New s7 is the D18 catch, with cue `catch`; the 'still growing?' hatching and the saturation-flag formula moved to it.
   - "the published threshold defines" (a threshold defines nothing) became "published runs define", here and in r8.
   - The seat source changed from T:908 to T:912. T:912 is the plan's own data design: "Rm on both sides of the threshold for two values of Pm".
6. **10.8.s3: the refraction analogy pointed the wrong way.**
   - Light entering glass bends toward the normal. B entering high-μ iron bends away from it, and the scene's own visual draws it that way.
   - Now: "field lines bend sharply at the iron's surface".
7. **10.8 "How to impose it is still open" overstated.**
   - Projection onto the vacuum-matching space is exact but costs one exterior solve per sample (Remark 2.5, T:266).
   - The open sub-question is doing it without that solve (T:114).
   - Now: "Imposing it exactly and cheaply, for every sample, is still open."
   - The 30-word nonlocal sentence was split. New cue `nonlocal` drives the rays of 'one point depends on the whole wall', which were crammed onto 'vacuum'.
   - A 0.5 s hold after the iron paragraph lets the kink be read.
8. **10.2.s6 omitted the cluster.**
   - s3 says the ovens run "on a laptop or a cluster". A real Slurm cluster was never run (C:45, C:190), and the chip says so.
   - Now: "never yet on a graphics card or a real cluster".
9. **10.1.s4: the lower steps were excluded from the proposal.** "From that step up, the plan is an independent PhD proposal" implied tiers 0-1 are not part of it. The whole ladder is the proposal (T:68). Now: "From there up, every step is a bet, written down before the data exist, in an independent PhD proposal."
10. **10.6.s4: "seed noise" became "starting noise".**
    - "Seed" already means the seed field (10.5) and the random seeds (10.4).
    - ch06 and ch07 call it noise ("a mirrored noise point always lands on the mirrored snapshot").
11. **10.7 result: overclaim on lamp (b).**
    - Lamp (b) uses `err_spectre_*_log10`, the mean |Δlog10 E| over shells k = 1-8 (`milady/eval/metrics.py`:600, 630-639). It is not the plan's per-band rule (T:917).
    - "every training spectrum sits inside the band" could be visibly false on screen. It is now "the spectrum error stays under a tenth of a decade at every training setting".
    - not_met: "At least one lamp is red; the screen shows which, and why." The old line blamed the generator, but lamp (a) can fail on precision alone (item 13).
    - "the branch test" became "the fourth lamp": "branch" is never introduced in the core cut.
12. **10.3 and 10.4 results: causes asserted from a check.**
    - 10.3: "The runs have settled" / "had not settled" became "The runs pass" / "At least one setting fails: its runs wait until the cause is found". A mean-budget miss can come from a transient, sampling or a bug.
    - 10.4 r6: "our resolution" became "the resolution" (BRIEF §6, "we").
    - 10.4 r7: "longer runs are needed" became "the sign cannot be read yet". Inconclusive has other causes, such as missing points or oscillating modes (R:1114).
13. **Precision of the 3D generator (new finding; visual, lamp rule, notes).**
    - fm3d decodes and returns float32 (`milady/train/data.py` `to_grid`, `fm3d.py`:782). `tests/test_train.py`:113 only asserts a relative divergence < 1e-6 in float32.
    - Two consequences follow:
      - The 10.6 gauge would read about 1e-7, not the 1e-16 its 'rounding error' band assumed.

    - The 10.6 band is now placed from `g.meta.dtype`. Lamp (a)'s rule now reads "by construction (previous scene), in double precision". Unit notes, request (5), asks for a float64 decode.
14. **10.9.s3: error-bar wording.** "the simulations' error bars" became "the simulated count's error bars". This is T:938's 95 % Poisson interval of the simulated count, worded as in X6.4.
