## Changes

1. **04.4.s8: "So the forecast fades before a typical flip arrives" became "So the forecast fades before the average wait ends".**
   - The horizon is about 155 units (11.2 × ln 10⁶, P4:815, P4:1778). The mean wait between crossings at Γ = 0.9 is 226 (1/0.00442, D:101).
   - Waits are Poissonian at long times (R:159), so the median wait is about 0.69 × 226 ≈ 157. That is the horizon itself: about half of the next crossings arrive *inside* it. The writer's own note says so, but "a typical flip" claimed the opposite.
   - The site compares the horizon with the *mean* interval (P4:1627). The narration now does the same.
   - **Visual of 04.4.** On 'decimal', the horizon now steps right twice, not three times. Three steps of about 26 units take it from 155 to about 233, past the 226 wait mark, which would contradict the narration on screen. Two steps reach about 207.

2. **04.3.s2: "Each tick is a flip, or a brief excursion that crosses and comes straight back" became "Not every tick is a flip: an excursion can cross and come straight back".**
   - An excursion that crosses and comes back makes *two* ticks, not one. The scene's own visual says so ("two ticks, the polarity is unchanged"), and so do 05.4.s2 and R:528.
   - The old either/or wording said each tick is one or the other.
   - "Can" keeps the claim at the level of R:528, which says the count *includes* excursions without saying how many there are.
   - **Visual of 04.3.** The strip must contain a real excursion, found by a seeded search. If none is found, it shows a SCHEMATIC inset rather than pretending the strip has one.

3. **04.3.s3: "crossing rate" now carries `{metric}…{/}`.** This matches 05.2.s7 "Out comes the {metric}crossing rate{/}" and 03.7's amber "how often". The film defines the term here (critique_pedagogy D5).
