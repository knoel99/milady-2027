## Checked against the sources (no change needed)

- **Definition 3.2.** The linear path, the loss J(v) and the minimizer v* = E[x1 − x0 | x_t, λ] match R:282-294. The fact that x0 is drawn independently of x1 ("pair each noise point with a random snapshot") is in R:289. The one-line regression argument is at R:296, and Lipman et al. 2023 is credited there for the transport.
- **E1 conditioning.** c = (Γ − 0.78)/0.24 with Fourier features (R:593). Heun with 50 steps and 100 network evaluations (R:595-601). Model A costs about 0.25 s per thousand samples (R:885).
- **Figure 4 (R:605).** The horizontal axis is the flow time. Straight paths cross, and the marginal field is the mean of their directions. At a fixed parameter, the trajectories do not cross. Non-crossing is stated only in 06.3, in the (t, x) plane (critique C9).
- **E1 protocol.** `data/e1_eval.json` gives 11 training values (0.80 to 1.00) and 10 test values (0.81 to 0.99), as at R:743. `data/e1_samples_0.93.json` holds 4,000 model A snapshots (seed 0, p_positive 0.497) and 8,000 reference snapshots (R:1330).
- **Interpolation.** P7:1182 (Remark 5.3) says success between the training values rests on regularity and inductive bias, not on a theorem. Nothing was tested beyond the range (R:743).
- **Noise floor.** R:713 (no true twin), R:715-723 (energy distance, the estimator's floor is strictly positive, compare at equal size, never with zero) and R:747 (the E1 floor is optimistic because its samples overlap; the E2 honest floor uses an independent simulation).
- **Toy wind.** The closed-form marginal field in the 06.1 visual was re-derived for two Gaussians at ±m with width s. It matches (1 − c t = (1 − t)/σ_t²). `data/fig_flow_matching.json` has every key the visuals read: cloud0 (130 points), cloud1 (140), pairs (34), fan (t 0.4, x 0.3, 7 strings, 2 weights), field (108 samples), traj.lam2 (10), x0b, pdf_noise and pdf_law2.
- **Question card evidence.** `milady/eval/metrics.py:650-652` builds the 3D floor from the two halves of the reference at one point. `infra/configs/palier2_tg3d.yaml` keeps 40 snapshots per point (t_keep 40, every 1.0, n_traj 1). `train_fm3d.yaml` scores 64 generated snapshots against at most 128 reference snapshots. So the floor is 20 consecutive snapshots against 20, and the score is 64 against 40. No topology metric exists (R:739, R:1039, no hit in `metrics.py`). Helicities are already computed (`metrics.py:210-219`). The plan's criteria are mixed: twice the Monte Carlo floor for tier 3 (T:938) and a fixed 0.1 decade for tier 2 (T:917).
- **Numbering.** n = 7 is consistent with ch05.Q = 6 and ch07.Q = 8.

## Changes

1. **06.2.s4.** "not the flow velocity" became "they are not the fluid's velocity".
   - The site calls v_θ "the velocity field of the flow" (R:260) and "the flow's velocity field" (T:167).
   - So "not the flow velocity" said that v_θ is not v_θ.
   - "Fluid's velocity" is the blue u that the scene contrasts it with.
2. **06.4.s4.** "On the three-dial bench, it trained at the filled dots" became "On the three dials, a real network trained at the filled dots".
   - "It" followed the toy's "One network, every setting", so it said that the toy network was trained on E1. The toy has an exact wind and no network (its chip says so).
   - E1's model A is a separate network.
   - "Three dials" is the wording of 04.1 and 07.1. "Three-dial bench" was used nowhere else.
3. **06.4.s6, split into s6 and s7 (new cue `beyond`).** "Working in between is a hope, not a theorem; beyond, untested" became "In between, no theorem promises success: only tests can tell. Beyond the range, nothing was tested."
   - The old line came right after "tested at the open dots", which are themselves in between. It sounded like a contradiction.
   - Its elliptic "beyond, untested" was hard to follow by ear.
   - The new wording keeps P7 Remark 5.3 and R:743. In the visual, the hatching moves to the new cue.
4. **06.4.s8 (was s7).** "its snapshots" became "the network's snapshots". After the new s7, "its" had no antecedent.
5. **06.5.s3, split into s3 and s4 (new cue `earth`).** "A distribution distance scores how much sand must move, and how far" became "A distribution distance scores how different the two piles are. The earth-mover kind counts how much sand must move, and how far."
   - The old line defined every distribution distance as the earth-mover cost.
   - The scores the film quotes later (×9 in 07.5, the floors of ch07-ch09) are energy distances, which are not transport costs (R:715-723). Only the sliced Wasserstein of E1 is (R:725).
   - The W1 formula moved to the `earth` cue.
6. **06.5 technical chip.** "energy distance (used in the experiments) · Wasserstein W1 (earth-mover)" became "energy distance (main score of E1 and E2) · Wasserstein W1, earth-mover (sliced in E1)".
   - The old chip implied that W1 was not used. E1 also reports sliced W1 (R:725).
7. **06.5.s8.** "That gap is the noise floor." became "That gap is the noise floor, about what a perfect model scores."
   - The predict beat asks what a perfect model scores, and the answer "No" was never tied back to the die.
   - The new clause pays off the predict beat explicitly (pedagogy K16).
8. **06.5.s10, split into s10 and s11 (new cue `overlap`).** The 19-word subtitle became two sentences. The visual's floor drop now starts on `overlap`.
9. **06.5 formula layer.** P, Q became P₁, P₂ (energy distance, W1, estimator bias).
   - Q is the quadrupole dial of 04.1, which 06.4's cards show 30 s earlier. The symbol sheet allows one meaning per mark.
10. **06.1.s1-s2.** "Start from pure noise" became "New snapshots start as pure noise". "The shape we want: the snapshots" became "the crowd we want: one point per snapshot". The label became "the snapshots we want · one point each".
    - The new s1 answers 05.5's open question ("how do you get snapshots…") in the first line.
    - The old s2 never said that a point is a whole snapshot, which is what the plane means. "Crowd" is the word of 04.7 and 06.5.
11. **06.1 formula layer.** "(transport time)" was added after t: 0 → 1.
    - 06.1 shows t before 06.2's warning. This was the order problem of critique C6.
12. **06.3.s10.** "against this clock, they never cross" became "drawn against the clock, they never cross".
    - "Against the clock" heard alone means "in a hurry". "Drawn against" names the plot.
13. **06.3 params.** n_strings went from 1,500 to 2,400 (alpha from 0.1 to 0.07). The narration says "Thousands of strings", and the old visual drew 1,534.
14. **ch06.Q.**
    - **Wording.** The on-screen text is now aligned with the spoken one ("the generated fields", 17 words).
    - **`why`.** It now says "as configured" and adds that the floor is not at the score's sample size (20/20 against 64/40). That is the "equal sample size" lesson of 06.5.
    - **`unblocks`.** It drops "helicities", which `metrics.py:210-219` already computes, and "spread of budgets", which is ch07.Q's question. It now reads: metrics to code (topology missing), the thresholds against the floor, and whether each held-out setting needs a second, independent run.
