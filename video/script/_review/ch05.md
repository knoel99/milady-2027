## Changes

1. **05.1.s2** "You have no clock, just one photo taken from above, where every speedometer can be read" became "No clock. Just one photo from above, where every speedometer can be read."
   - The new wording is shorter and easier to follow by ear. It frees the words used in changes 2 and 3.
2. **05.1.s5** "Fast cars rush through it, so each one caught inside counts for more" became "A fast car spends little time inside, so catching one there counts for more."
   - The new sentence states the reason for weighting by speed: a fast car spends little time in the strip. Without that reason, the recipe arrives before the viewer knows why it works (pedagogy K10).
3. **05.1 paragraph 3 split in two (strip, fast | sum, rate); 05.1.s6** "Add up their speeds" became "Add up the speeds of the cars inside".
   - The split puts a pause between weighting and summing. In that pause the visual stacks the speed bars and steps the width brace.
   - After the pause, "their" had no antecedent, so the sentence now names the cars.
   - The scene now runs about 42.9 s against a 40 s target (+7 %).
4. **05.2.s6** "divide by its width and by the number of photos" became "… by the total number of photos". The on-screen recipe label and the pen beat in the visual were changed to match.
   - "The number of photos" right after "the speeds inside the strip" can be heard as the number of photos in the strip. Dividing by that count gives the mean speed in the strip divided by the width, which is wrong.
   - The estimator divides by N, all the samples (R:703-707).
5. **05.2.s8** "In the limit of a vanishing strip, it is exact for well-behaved systems" became "Shrink the strip to nothing, and for well-behaved systems the recipe is exact: Rice's formula."
   - "It" was ambiguous. It could mean the strip, the rate or the recipe.
   - "Well-behaved" now sets up the callback in change 8.
6. **05.2 formula layer, item 1.** ∫|u̇| p(0, u̇) du̇ became ∫|y| p_{D,Ḋ}(0, y) dy.
   - The symbol sheet (outline §2) reserves u for the fluid velocity.
   - The report's dummy variable u (R:514) would clash with the blue u of ch06 and ch08.
7. **05.3.s2** "count the crossings in time" became "count the crossings as they happen". **05.3.s5** "the area under the true curve" became "the area under the counted curve".
   - "In time" can be heard as "punctually".
   - The on-screen definition says "counted curve", and 09.1 reuses the same device, so the narration now uses the same word.
8. **05.3.s7** "where the formula's fine print is not even guaranteed" became "that are not even guaranteed to be well-behaved".
   - "Fine print … guaranteed" is an odd phrase.
   - The new wording calls back 05.2.s8. It keeps the caveat of R:528, R:883 and R:901: the density assumption of Proposition 8 is not guaranteed for deterministic chaos.
9. **05.3 y axis in crossings per 1,000 time units, 0–13, ticks 0, 4, 8, 12, scale 1000, as in 04.3.**
   - The old axis was "crossing rate of D (per unit time)", with ticks 0.005 and 0.01.
   - The viewer saw this counted curve build in 04.3, where the narration says "two ticks per thousand time units… about eleven". The y title and the label changed accordingly.
   - The values per unit time stay on the formula layer. The Rice curve peaks at 12.2 per 1,000, so it fits under 13.
10. **05.4.s3** "counts crossings of a level farther from zero" became "counts crossings of a far level, on the other side".
    - Any excursion from the upper polarity dips below +a: the drawn trace goes from 0.8 to 0.55 with a = 0.6. So "a level farther from zero" would count it too.
    - Proposition 2.30 defines a reversal as a passage from D ≥ a to D ≤ −a, and bounds r_{+→−} with the crossings of −a (T:684-687). The far level is the one on the other side.
    - The visual now tells the builder not to mark the excursion's dip below +a.
11. **05.4 formula layer.** "r_a: crossings of ±a" became "r_a: crossing rate of the level a, equal to that of −a for a mirror-symmetric law". The definition "reversal: from D ≥ a to D ≤ −a" was added.
    - Counting both levels would double the rate, and the bound r ≤ r_a would then be wrong by a factor of 2 (T:684).
12. **05.5.s2** "Yet full simulations exist at only a few knob settings" became "Yet real dynamo simulations exist at only a few knob settings".
    - Twenty seconds earlier, 05.3 said that simulations ran at forty-one settings of Γ, so the old sentence contradicted it.
    - The scarcity concerns 3D dynamo runs (T:62), which is what 03.6 sets up and what this scene calls back.
13. **ch05.Q.** The question became "For VKS, which dipole should Rice count, and what threshold turns a crossing into a reversal?" (16 words, about 9.3 s). The why, the unblocks text and their French versions were rewritten and shortened.
    - The old unblocks text said "which dipole series every run must log at each step". The periodic 3D solver logs no dipole series (`milady/sim/mhd3d.py:639-680`).
    - In a periodic box, the plan's Rice metric is threshold crossings of the magnetic energy (T:916). The dipole question belongs to tier 3: the VKS simulation data of Bousquet et al. 2026, with its dipolar POD mode, and criterion (d) (T:645, T:933-938).
    - The question now says so. The unblocks text now names the data request: the dipole series, plus u and B together on each snapshot, because Ḋ needs both (Proposition 2.28).
    - "Near-misses" became "excursions", the word 04.3 and 05.4 use.
    - VKS is pinned in `audio/lexicon.json`.
14. **`notes`** now carries a one-line summary of this review.

## Remaining doubts

- **`data/ch05_rice.json` does not exist yet.** It must be written by the foundation engineer, following the spec in the visuals of 05.2 and 05.3.
  - Until then, the assembler renders 05.2 and 05.3 as pending.
  - Every value in it can be recomputed from existing files, and no run is needed.
- **`data/e1_finegrid.json` `bridge` block.** The block is labelled P(|D| < h) with h_rice = 0.2, but its masses use 0.25 (`experiences/e1_gissinger.py:373`, `:437`).
  - ch05 does not read that block.
  - If someone fixes it by setting h_rice to 0.25, no ch05 scene is affected, because ch05 takes h from `data/ch05_rice.json`.
- **The (D, Ḋ) plane of 05.2 has a signed range of ±4.** It shows every point, since the extremes are −3.2 and +3.5. Yet 99 % of the points lie within ±1.34, so the cloud fills only about a third of the height.
  - The builder may prefer ±2.5 with edge markers for the 24 points beyond. That is a layout choice, so I did not change it.
