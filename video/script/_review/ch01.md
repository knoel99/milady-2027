4. **01.3.s10: "each turn multiplies a tiny seed" became "each turn multiplies a tiny seed field by the same factor: exponential growth".**

   - The old source line said "a permanent magnet turning in a coil", and the visual showed the magnet spinning. Both now show the coil turning around a fixed magnet. The narration ("using a built-in magnet") is unchanged.

   - The key point under P3 Theorem 3.3 (P3:491-498) holds only in a statistically steady regime. During growth, which is exactly what 01.3 just showed, the field stores energy.
