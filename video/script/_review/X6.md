## Changes (what, why, evidence)

1. **"Bet four" and "Bet five" were spoken, but no "Bet three" exists.**
   - The film says "Bet one" (10.4, X1.3) and "Bet two" (10.5), and nothing else. 10.7 and X3.5 are not numbered. A listener would hear a gap.
   - The narration now says "The VKS bet" and "The DRESDYN bet" (X6.4.s1, X6.4.r1, X6.5.s1, X6.5.r1).
   - The titles are now "Bet: VKS in the generator" and "Bet: DRESDYN, the precession knob", in the same style as X3.5 ("Bet: the crowd law in 3D"). No other file uses the old titles (grep).
2. **X6.4.s1 overclaim: "puts the sodium experiment inside the generator".**
   - Tier 3 trains on the partners' SFEMaNS/LATU *simulations* of VKS, not on experimental data (T:929, T:933; R:161; S:98).
   - New text: "The VKS bet would train a generator on the partners' simulations of the sodium experiment." The conditional keeps the scene honest as PLANNED work.
3. **X6.4.s3 was one 35-word sentence listing four criteria.**
   - It was the longest sentence of the unit and hard to follow by ear.
   - It is now two sentences. "bet" covers lamps a and b, and a new cue "iron" covers lamps c and d. The ids are renumbered s1-s6, and the visual says which lamps light on which cue.
   - "noise floor" is now marked `{metric}`, as in ch06 and ch07.
   - To make room, "no reader for them exists yet" became "no file reader exists yet", and the target went from 42 to 45 s (the outline gives 45 s with results).
4. **X6.4 result variant.**
   - r3 ("Against criteria written before the data arrived") said the same thing as r7 ("the criteria were fixed in advance"). It is now "Against the plan's four criteria: {{verdict}}."
   - r5 implied that the plan has one generic next step. T:939 names a fallback for criterion (c) only: "decoder by material subdomains". The new text is "if it is the iron rule, the plan switches to a decoder built material by material". Source T:939 is added.
   - r6: "often because" became "for instance when". It reports a single result, not a recurring event.
   - The result target is now 43 s.
5. **X6.5.r3 was factually wrong: "At the ratio never simulated".**
   - Criterion (a) compares Rm_c from generated flows with Rm_c from simulated flows at the held-out Po (T:952). That Po must therefore be simulated; it is only kept out of training.
   - New text: "At the held-out ratio, unseen in training". r1 "trained between them" became "trained on them".
6. **X6.5.r5 overstated the plan: "the plan's next step is to condition on the branch".**
   - Branch conditioning is an *ablation* in T:950 (Remark 2.37), not a stopping rule. The text is now "one remedy the plan lists is…", with source T:950 added.
   - r6 started with "Most likely", which guessed at a reason, and named "the lamp" when the curve seat has two lamps. It is now "The data could not decide, for instance because…".
7. **X6.5 wording for the ear.**
   - s2 "how fast the axis turns against the spin" can be heard as "turning in the opposite direction". It is now "relative to the spin", because Po is a frequency ratio (P3:1208, T:941).
   - s3 had an ambiguous "it" (the solver or the flow?). It is now "the field that flow would grow".
   - The target went from 37 to 38 s (83 words).
8. **X6.3.s3 left out the m = 0 mode.**
   - "waves of one, two, three bumps" skipped the axisymmetric part. That part carries the VKS dipole: the four dominant POD modes are axisymmetric (R:161, S:98), and Remark 2.9 (T:341) sums over every m.
   - New text: "waves of zero, one, two, three bumps per turn". The visual now has an m = 0 ring (uniform), `params.rings.m = [0, 1, 2, 3]` and the label 'm = 0'.
