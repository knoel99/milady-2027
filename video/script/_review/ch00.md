## Changes

1. **00.1.s2: "remembers more" became "remembers further back".**
   - "More" left the time frame unclear, so "North and south have swapped" could be heard as happening now. "Further back" makes the swap a past event, and the chip "illustration, not to time scale" covers the visual.
   - Side effect: the flip cue moves from about 6.3 s to about 6.75 s, closer to the 7 s set by BRIEF D20.

2. **00.4.s9: "They must learn which laws hold exactly…" became "They must obey some laws exactly, and others only on average."**
   - The machines do not learn which laws are exact. In the report and the thesis, the designer imposes each law at the level where it holds: per sample through the architecture, or on the distribution (T:64; R:1201).
   - The old line misstated the film's central lesson.

3. **00.4.s8: "machines that dream up magnetic fields" became "…dream up snapshots of magnetic fields".**
   - The teaser cloud is made of E1 three-mode snapshots (D, Q), not fields.
   - The new wording plants "snapshot", the key word of ch04 to ch06 ("shoebox", "A machine that dreams snapshots"), and stays true to the visual.

4. **00.4 formula layer, item 1.** `T_Lyap ≈ 11 ≪ 1/r_0 > 100 (Γ ≤ 0.95)` became `T_Lyap ≈ 11 ≪ 1/r_0 (1/r_0 > 100 for Γ ≤ 0.95)`.
   - The old chain "≪ … >" was malformed. The new form follows P4:1627: mean interval above 100 for Γ ≤ 0.95, Lyapunov time 1/λ₁ ≈ 11.
   - λ stays off screen, as the symbol sheet requires.

5. **00.4 chips.**
   - The NOAA chip "no one can say when yet" became "no one can say if or when". The NCEI FAQ says "we cannot state for certain if or when a magnetic reversal will occur"; "yet" implied a coming capability.
   - The violet chip "generated · E1 three-mode model, Γ = 0.9" became "generated snapshots · E1 three-mode model". The L0 ladder allows no symbols on screen (pedagogy §0.1). Γ = 0.9 stays in the data provenance stamp.

6. **00.4 visual note and sources.**
   - "Rice recovers the crossing rate from snapshots" became "from snapshots plus the equations, cumulative error" (critique_accuracy A4; R:507, R:528).
   - Added the source line R:507/:528 (needs F, counts every crossing).

7. **00.2 dipole tilt: params.dipole_tilt_deg changed from 11 to 9.4.**
   - The NOAA NCEI FAQ, checked 2026-11-08, says "currently inclined at 9.41°". "Approximately 11 degrees" is the older value given on the same page.
   - The source line now quotes both values. The URL is updated to the live page: ngdc.noaa.gov/geomag/faqgeom.shtml redirects with a 301 to ncei.noaa.gov/products/geomagnetism-frequently-asked-questions. The same URL fix is applied in 00.4.

8. **00.3 sources.**
   - The Tauxe Table 15.1 values were checked online. The table is "Composite GPTS of Gee & Kent (2007)", chapter 15 "The GPTS and Magnetostratigraphy". All 12 normal intervals in `params.normal_Ma` match it.
   - The count of 22 boundaries between 0 and 5 Ma is confirmed.
   - Added a Gee & Kent 2007 reference and the NOAA facts "spaced 5 thousand to 50 million years" and "last reversal about 750,000-780,000 years ago", which support "wildly uneven gaps".

9. **00.5 chip: "not endorsed by MilaDy" became "not produced or endorsed by MilaDy".** This follows the D14 wording more closely (outline 03.7; H:45 "neither produced nor endorsed").

10. **ch00.Q question.**
    - Old: "…or warning signs of the *next* flip?" New: "…or the warning signs *just before* a flip?" (18 words on screen).
    - Reason: 00.4 had just called "when is the next flip" hopeless, so asking about warning signs of the next flip sounded like a contradiction. The precursor phase of MilaDy objective (i) is short-range (S:72, S:76). The pen underline moves to "just before".

    - hold_after changed from 1.0 to 0.8 s. target_s changed from 9 to 10 s, still within the 6-10 s the brief allows: an 18-word card plus the WHY and UNBLOCKS reveals needs about 10 s on screen.
