# DELIVERY

- Report: `REPORT.md` (results table in `results/table.md`, figures `results/fig_spectra.png`, `results/fig_fields.png`).
- Code: `src/` — `mhd2d.py` (2-D pseudo-spectral MHD solver), `gen_data.py` (dataset), `fm.py` (conditional flow matching, strategies M0–M5), `evalm.py` (metrics), `eval_all.py`, `figs.py`. (`gen_data_parallel_UNUSED.py` was not used.)
- Data: `results/data.npz` (3200 simulations of 32x32 2-D MHD, regenerated by `gen_data.py`; 52 MB, kept local, not on the Hub: it is trivially regenerable). No models published: the networks are under-trained toy models (see limits in the report), so nothing is pushed to the Hugging Face Hub.
- Compliance matrix: `COMPLIANCE.md`; decisions: `DECISIONS.md`; NeurIPS checklist: `CHECKLIST.md`; citation: `CITATION.cff`; costs: `COSTS.md` (written by the framework at delivery).
