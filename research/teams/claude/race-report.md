## Measured

- Zero divergence is exact by design (vector potentials) or by projection (1e-8); a divergence penalty leaves 0.26 and doubles the distance to the reference law.
- Every model of the first protocol is 117 to 519 noise floors from the reference in energy distance. The same network with an explicit mode-wise affine term in the velocity field (W9) is at 9.3 floors (factor 22 against its matched control at 205).
- In energy distance, no learned model beats two Gaussian laws without a network. Per Fourier mode (G0): 3.5 floors, but no magnetic production (magnetic balance residual 0.998). With the covariance between modes (G1, fitted on the training η): 2.5 floors, magnetic balance 0.072, null-count error 1.10 against 1.66 for W9; confirmed on a second realisation of the data. W9 has a lower residual of the magnetic balance on the mean over η (0.055), which is not an advantage: it comes from two errors that cancel (next item).
- The reference law is non-Gaussian in its magnetic production. Split exactly into a mean-flow part (second-order statistics) and a third-order part, the production of the reference has a third-order part of +0.054, −0.137 and −0.270 of the Ohmic dissipation at the three held-out η (+0.14 to −0.31 over the eleven η). The Gaussian laws have none (below 0.002). W9 has −0.006, −0.082 and −0.118: 0.60 and 0.44 of the reference at the two lower η, in every seed and on both data realisations (H25, which predicted none, is refuted), and a small value of the opposite sign at the largest. Its mean-flow part is 10 to 16 % low at the held-out η: the magnetic balance of W9 (production over dissipation 0.79, 0.93, 1.02) is the sum of a mean-flow error of −0.15, −0.13, −0.13 and a third-order error of −0.06, +0.06, +0.15, which cancel at the two lower η and add at the largest.
- The restart drift of objective T1 (relative to each ensemble's own initial energy, at τ = 4) does not rank the laws. Anchored on the reference energy and followed to τ = 16, the six laws tested that start on the reference magnetic energy (within 0.04) or below it (G0, G1, G2, G3, W9, W11) gain 10 to 21 % of it after the restart (W10, which starts 21 % above, relaxes downwards; that the rises have one cause is not established): W9 starts 12 % low and comes back to the reference (overshoot 0.0045, |a(16)| = 0.010); G1 and G0 start on it and overshoot it by 9 % and 17 % before coming back towards it (H26 confirmed on its registered criterion, both realisations; it does not show that W9 is closer to the attractor than G1). The largest distance to the reference energy over the course is comparable for W9 and G1 (0.13 and 0.10).
- On the measure registered for H27 (the largest deviation from the reference energy beyond the initial one), the Gaussian law with the mean and covariance of 8192 W9 samples (G2) does not move farther than W9 (H27 refuted on the second data realisation; on the first the null control of the test missed its threshold by 0.004 and the test is invalid as written). That measure cannot see a passage above the reference smaller than the 12 % initial deficit. G2 loses the third-order production, has a null-count error higher by 0.04 ± 0.03 out of 1.7, is 0.43 floors from W9 in energy distance (about twice the sampling value) and 0.6 to 0.9 floors farther from the reference; its drift at τ = 4 is 0.170 against 0.124 for W9 (paired +0.045 ± 0.007, both realisations). Of the metrics of the first protocol, the energy distance and the null count hardly see the non-Gaussian part of W9; the restart drift does.
- G1, the Gaussian law with the moments of the reference, has 2.1 times its null points on the mean over η, at the training η as at the held-out ones (2.5 times at the largest η, 1.6 at the smallest); at the held-out η, the only ones where the restart was run and where G1 is interpolated, it leaves the stationary energy by 9 to 11 %.
- A law-level stationarity loss lowers the residual of the energy identity at η without data (0.29 → 0.19 on a wrong model, 0.057 → 0.029 on a nearly right one), not that of the identity E[⟨j·B⟩] = 0, and on the nearly right model it raises the distance from 9.3 to 16.4 floors. Exponential tilting on the same identities collapses on the wrong model and improves the nearly right one on most metrics (energy residual 0.011, effective sample 17 %).
- Longer training (8×) and wider networks do not close the gap: the loss falls and the law gets worse (W9: 9.3 → 18.8 floors; plain network: 205 floors at width 512, 25.6 at width 2048, 104 at width 5632).
- No law within 60 floors of the reference has the reference number of magnetic null points (2.1 to 3.5 times on the mean over η; the reference count on 400 snapshots differs by 4 to 10 % between two draws), and none is stationary under the solver.
- A network of the size of W9 anchored on the exact Gaussian field of G1 learns nothing more in the same budget (H28 refuted on both realisations: third-order part below 0.005 of the reference on the seed means (0.023 in one seed) at every checkpoint, loss on a fixed training sample unchanged to 3e-5). The positive control fails too (H38 refuted): trained on 14 400 samples of W9, the same anchored model recovers 0.006 and −0.002 of the third-order part of W9, while W9 retrained from scratch on these samples recovers 0.32 and 0.44. The negative result of H28 belongs to the anchored construction or its optimisation, not to the data; its cause is not identified. With an anchor of full rank (one block per Fourier mode plus the 300 leading inter-mode directions of the sample covariance) the network trains (loss on a fixed training sample 1.208 → 1.151) and the law moves away from its anchor (2.5 → 16.4 floors, magnetic energy 11 to 15 % too high), and no third-order production appears, on the data or on samples of W9 (H40 refuted on its three clauses, both realisations): the rank deficiency of the sample covariance is not a sufficient explanation. The code shared by these models is cleared for a block-diagonal anchor (H41 not refuted: at rank 0 it reproduces W10, the model anchored on the mode-wise law through the earlier code, within 1.3e-4 of the dissipation; a fault specific to an anchor with dense eigenvectors is not excluded). Reported with it, one realisation and one intermediate rank: a third-order part (0.31 and 0.24 of the reference at the two lower held-out η) is measured only with the mode-wise anchor; with 30 or 300 inter-mode directions, or the full sample covariance, none is (seed means below 0.002, at most 0.005 in any seed); no cause is established, and neither anchored model is a good law (44 floors, magnetic energy 10 to 28 % too high).
- The third-order production of W9 is not produced by the model from Gaussian data: trained on independent Gaussian draws with the two moments of the data (H33) or on Gaussian trajectories with the per-direction autocorrelation of the data (H34, first clause), the same model has none (below 0.004 of the dissipation in any seed). Trained on independent draws it has no such deficit of magnetic energy (at most 5.3 %) but still misses 0.06 to 0.10 of the dissipation in mean-flow production, about half of what the model trained on the data misses; on correlated trajectories its magnetic energy is 8 to 15 % too high at most η (the second clause of H34, written on absolute values, is refuted by its letter).
- The deficit of magnetic energy of W9 is not reduced by taking the same 2400 snapshots per η from 96 trajectories instead of 24 (H36, second cycle: 0.159, 0.097, 0.141; thin margin at one η; the number of independent states is not measured).
- Recolouring at sampling time (H29): the samples of W9 sent through the optimal-transport linear map from the moments of the model onto those of G1 (W16) hold 0.74 and 0.58 of the third-order production of the reference at the two lower held-out η (0.79 and 0.59 on the second realisation), with the mean-flow production within 0.017 of the reference, the magnetic energy within 1.3 %, a magnetic balance residual of 0.034 (G1: 0.072) and 2.07 floors (G1: 2.55; worse than G1 at the largest η). Energy balance and spectrum are those of the copied moments. It comes from the data: the same model trained on Gaussian surrogates and recoloured the same way (W17) has the excursion (0.101), the restart drift (0.061 against 0.021) and the magnetic balance (0.071 against 0.034) of the Gaussian law (H37, both realisations); of the 0.4 to 0.5 floor that W16 gains over G1, 0.3 is lost on surrogate training, and the null-count error changes by 0.04 to 0.05 only. ## Conjectured

Why the anchored models hold no third-order production when their anchor carries 30 or 300 inter-mode directions, or the full sample covariance (the rank deficiency of the sample covariance is ruled out as a sufficient cause by H40; no other cause is tested); that the excess of null points of the Gaussian and recoloured laws is a missing alignment of the small scales of B by the flow, rebuilt by the solver in a few time units (conjecture of the `physicien` role; only the relaxation is measured); why the model trained on the data loses 11 to 16 % of magnetic energy that the same model keeps on Gaussian data; that the failure of plain networks at large width or long training is memorisation of the 14 400 training snapshots; that the ranking of the mechanisms carries over to turbulent regimes. Literature searches found no generative model of dynamo simulations and no use of the helicity identity, null counts or a restart test for generative models: negative statements, not proofs.

## The internship offer: what is covered

The requirements of the offer (`docs/specs/sources/supervisors.md` §1.2 a) are taken one by one in the table of the introduction of the report, with the statuses that the `jury-externe` review of 14 November accepted or corrected. Covered: constraints by design, loss and sampling (eleven mechanisms, with an unconstrained baseline, a diffusion model and three Gaussian laws); zero divergence; spectra, helicity and divergence as metrics; the comparative study, with the restriction that twelve of its sixteen lines are models more than 100 floors from the reference. Partly: the generative models (flow matching throughout, one diffusion model of the first generation; no learned model as trained is closer to the reference than a Gaussian law); the conditioning (η only, Rm 6.25 to 16.7, Re = 10, saturated regime); the energy balance (a law-level constraint; the best balance is that of second moments copied from the data at sampling time); the code (the package of this repository, not the toolbox of the consortium). Measured and not achieved: the preservation of topology (two to three times the null points of the reference without the solver). Not covered: polarity reversals (a snapshot generator fixes at most a mean rate of sign changes, not computed), the use of generated fields by a reduced-order model or a data assimilation (the reference fields are published in the format of the project, with the models and a script that writes generated fields in it), large data sets and the turbulent regime. The report lists what each would need (section "What the turbulent regime, the dynamo threshold and the data of the project would need").
