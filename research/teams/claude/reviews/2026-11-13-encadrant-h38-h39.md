- (a) Weights change (max |Δw| of the EMA 0.28 to 0.59, last layer non-zero). No silent failure.
- (b) The EMA is a distinct module with its own `base`; the half-way and final checkpoints differ; `load_state_dict` changes the loss. In the pod JSON, E_B differs between checkpoints at the 1e-5 level, so the states loaded are distinct.
- (c) The predicted contrast did not appear. With n >> D the anchored network learned nothing; with n < D it moved most, by memorising. The unanchored network did not learn the skew either, so the toy is not a positive control.
- What stands of the explanation: the loss figures (1.037 against 1.221 on the same fixed sample) are measured. The toy also shows the n < D anchor is strongly overfit (0.39 in-sample, 4.9 on fresh samples).
- What does not stand: "nothing is left to learn in-sample" is unmeasured, and the toy points the other way.
- Detail: n < D holds per training η only. At held-out η the anchor mixes two covariances, of rank up to 9600 > 5320.
