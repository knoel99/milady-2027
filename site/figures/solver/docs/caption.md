**Figure — The solver advances the spectral state with an integrating-factor RK4, and each simulation writes an MFF file whose parts each have their row in the catalog.** **a**, One step of `milady.sim.mhd3d`: the state $(\hat{\mathbf{u}}, \hat{\mathbf{B}})$ is stored as Fourier coefficients (modes outside the dealiasing cube stay zero; the glyph shows 11 points per direction, the actual cutoff is $|m| \le 21$ at $64^3$). Curls are taken in Fourier space, the products $\mathbf{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega} + \mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{B}$ and $\mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{B}$ in physical space (slice: $|\mathbf{u}|$ of the initial Taylor–Green flow); back in Fourier space, the 2/3 rule removes aliased modes, the Leray projection removes the gradient part of the momentum term (the pressure), and the induction term is a curl, hence solenoidal. The Lawson RK4 integrates the diffusion $\nu k^2$, $\eta k^2$ exactly; four right-hand-side evaluations per step. After the step come the random forcing (when chosen) and, periodically, a clean-up projection of $\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ and $\hat{\mathbf{B}}$: both divergences stay at machine precision. **b**, One simulation of `stage2_tg3d`: diagnostics every 0.1 from $t = 0$, 40 snapshots of $(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{B})$ over the kept window $]100, 140]$, a checkpoint every 900 s of compute and on SIGTERM (Slurm time limit, preemptible instance stopped), automatic resume; the position of the interruption is an example. **c**, The MFF file follows The Well's convention (grey) and adds `milady_*` attributes and groups; the identifier, parameters, commit and diagnostics have their column in the catalog, so the simulations part of the catalog can be rebuilt from the files alone.
