**Generative pipeline: the model generates the Fourier modes of the vector potentials, so every sample is divergence-free by construction.** Snapshots (u, B) on the 16^3 grid are Fourier transformed and the modes |m_i| <= 5 are kept (665 independent complex modes). Vector potentials are written in the Craya-Herring basis of the plane perpendicular to k, giving a latent of 5320 real numbers. A conditional flow-matching network, conditioned on log eta, learns the velocity along straight paths from noise to data; samples are obtained by integrating the flow, and B = curl A is decoded with zero divergence by construction. The mode lattice, point clouds and field lines are illustrative. All content comes from site/teams/claude/figures/FACTS.md.
