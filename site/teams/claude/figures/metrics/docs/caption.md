**Metrics computed on a generated ensemble against the reference ensemble at held-out η.** (a) Key: generated snapshots in green, reference (solver) snapshots in dark ink; velocity u blue, magnetic field B vermillion, forcing grey. (b) Energy distance on physical features, divided by the noise floor (drawn as a band), the mean over 10 splits of the reference trajectories into two halves of the distance between the two subsamples; a perfect generator would score about 0.6, not 1. (c) Relative divergence ‖∇·B‖/‖∇B‖, zero for a solenoidal field. (d) Mean energy balance: forcing injection against viscous and magnetic dissipation. (e) Shell spectrum E_B(k), k = 1…8, and mean magnetic helicity H_M. (f) Mean number of magnetic null points (B = 0). (g) Restart drift: generated snapshots are advanced by the solver over τ = 4 and the relative change of the generated ensemble's own mean magnetic energy, between t = 0 and t = τ, is read (bracket). (h) Polarity symmetry: the equations are invariant under B → −B, so P(⟨u·B⟩ > 0) should be ½. All curves, bars and positions are schematic and carry no measured value; the metric definitions come from `site/teams/claude/figures/FACTS.md`.
