## Structure
Skeleton: one principle with several cases. One shared glyph set (noise cloud, straight paths to samples, constraint surface "div B = 0" as an orange line, law-level target = diamond (mean of samples) and dashed ring, orange correction arrow); each cell reuses it and changes only what the mechanism changes. Three columns named as in the offer: Model design (W4, W4s, W9-W11), Training loss (W1, W5), Sampling procedure (W2, W3, W7, W8). Baselines have their own cells below the constraints, not as constraints: W0 ("Baseline: no constraint") and "Model families (baselines)" with G0 (no network) and W6 (diffusion, same network); the shared-glyph legend sits under Model design. W3 is labelled "training noise projected + every stage projected"; W7 shows the weights collapsing onto a single sample, with "measured: effective sample size 1 of 256 in 14 of 15 cases". Cells whose mechanism acts on the law (W9-W11, G0, W5, W7, W8) drop the surface and show the law-level target instead; W5 (a mean) and W8 (each sample) are deliberately contrasted.
Negative constraints: no result, no ranking, no efficacy claim (W1 and W2 are drawn without showing whether the constraint is enforced); the surface is a straight line only because div B = 0 is linear. Spatial layout of dots is illustrative.
Inferred: the energy identity text in W5 uses the colour key (f grey, u/omega blue, j orange).
