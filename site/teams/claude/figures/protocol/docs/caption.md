**Experimental protocol.** (a) A hypothesis is registered with its quantitative refutation criterion before the computation; the supervisor role then gives a verdict (confirmed, refuted or not discriminating). (b) The 11 values of the magnetic diffusivity η (0.08 to 0.03, log-spaced) alternate between 6 training values (indices 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10), 2 validation values (3, 7) and 3 held-out test values (1, 5, 9), which are used neither for training nor for choosing the model (the generator is chosen on the validation η, mean over the seeds); each η has 24 trajectories of 100 snapshots of (u, B). (c) Every variant is trained with 5 seeds, numbered 0 to 4 and compared by the paired difference over seeds (mean ± standard error); dot positions are schematic, not data. (d) The noise floor is the mean, over 10 splits of the 24 reference trajectories into two halves, of the energy distance between the two subsamples of 512 snapshots, and generators are reported as the energy distance to the reference divided by this floor (the position of the generator on the axis is illustrative).
