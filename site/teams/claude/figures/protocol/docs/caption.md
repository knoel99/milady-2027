**Experimental protocol.** (a) A hypothesis is registered with its quantitative refutation criterion before the computation; the supervisor role then gives a verdict (confirmed, refuted or not discriminating). (b) The 11 values of the magnetic diffusivity η (0.08 to 0.03, log-spaced) alternate between 6 training values (indices 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10), 2 validation values (3, 7) and 3 held-out test values (1, 5, 9), which are used neither for training nor for choosing the model; each η has 24 trajectories of 100 snapshots of (u, B). (c) Every variant is trained with 5 seeds and compared by the paired difference over seeds (mean ± standard error); dot positions are schematic, not data. (d) The noise floor is the energy distance between two subsamples of 512 snapshots drawn from disjoint halves of the 24 reference trajectories, and generators are reported as the energy distance to the reference divided by this floor (the position of the generator on the axis is illustrative).
