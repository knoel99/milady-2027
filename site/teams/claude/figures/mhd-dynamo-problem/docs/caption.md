**The parametric MHD problem: a forced helical dynamo whose stationary state depends on the magnetic diffusivity η.** (a) A constant helical force at wavenumber k_f = 2 (grey) drives a flow u (blue) in the periodic box [0, 2π)³; the saturated magnetic field B (vermillion) has a large-scale part at k = 1. (b) The control parameter η takes 11 log-spaced values from 0.08 to 0.03 (Rm = 1/(k_f η) from 6.25 to 16.7; dynamo threshold near Rm ≈ 5, exploratory); alternating values are used for training (6), validation (2) and held-out testing (3). Between the end values, the kinetic energy E_u falls (0.48 to 0.25), the magnetic energy E_B rises (0.08 to 0.17) and the magnetic helicity H_M becomes more negative (−0.044 to −0.10); straight segments show the direction only. (c) The governing equations in Alfvén units, with ν = 0.05 fixed (Re = 10). (d) The generative model, conditioned on η, must produce snapshots (u, B) of the stationary state at held-out η, to be compared with solver snapshots; the textured tiles are illustrative.
