**Why the first generators are over-dispersed, and what was tried.** (a) The optimal flow-matching velocity field splits into a linear part and the rest; for Gaussian data it is exactly affine. The networks of W0-W6 have width 512 for a 5320-dimensional latent (neck drawn to scale); we conjecture that a network narrower than the latent cannot carry the linear part (to be tested by the width scan, hypothesis H21). Measured: their flow-matching loss ends at 1.60-1.63, above pi/2 = 1.571, the loss of the exact linear field for independent unit Gaussian coordinates, and their samples have 2-3 times too many null points and dE/dt fluctuations 2-14 times the reference. (b) Four remedies on the same glyphs: G0 (Gaussian law per Fourier mode, no network), W9 (learned mode-wise affine term plus network), W10 (exact Gaussian field plus network) and W11 (exact Gaussian field, learned affine correction and network). Dots show the energy distance to the reference over the noise floor on held-out values of the magnetic diffusivity, for 2 seeds on CPU (five-seed results are pending and not shown); the grey band is W0-W6 (5 seeds, GPU).
