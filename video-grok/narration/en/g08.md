# g08 — A law without a clock

## g08.stationary

duration: 0:30
badge: PROVED

### cue.g08.stationary.show

role: spoken

A stationary law names the cloud of states. It does not put those states in order. You can shuffle the pictures and the law stays the same. Two snapshots can share the law and still refuse a clock. Knowing the cloud is not the same as knowing which picture comes next. A frequency has to start from that cloud.

## g08.rice

duration: 0:45
badge: PROVED

### cue.g08.rice.show

role: spoken

How often does the dipole cross zero? The rate can come from the law together with one observable, the quantity we count. Call that observable F. Nothing in the statement asks you to integrate a trajectory forward in time. The count looks through a window. Call the width of that window h. F has to be known. Every crossing counts, including excursions and the chatter around zero.

### cue.g08.rice.caveat

role: spoken

A continuous density of the pair, the dipole and its rate of change, is a hypothesis. E1 is deterministic. That density is not guaranteed. The proved statement does not cover the percentages.

### cue.g08.numbers.show

role: spoken

On the reference, the cumulative error is 4 percent near the threshold and 6 percent above it. On generator C the cumulative error is 14 percent for Gamma at or above 0.89. It reaches 65 percent at one point, at Gamma 0.905 and at Gamma 0.91. Near the threshold the cumulative error is 42 percent. The model lags, then it overshoots. Keep the single-point spike separate from the cumulative bars. They are not the same error. These percentages are zero crossings. They are not three-dimensional reversals.

### cue.g08.numbers.caveat

role: spoken

Those 4 percent and 6 percent figures are the numerical check of the window estimator. They are not the theorem under its hypotheses.

## g08.not

duration: 0:20
badge: PROVED

### cue.g08.not.show

role: spoken

This rate is not the law of waiting times. Waiting times would say how long a polarity lasts. This count does not. Excursions count, and so does the chatter around zero. An optional module, x03, goes further into that limit. The core does not depend on it.
