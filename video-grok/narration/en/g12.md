# g12 — The map of the field

## g12.learn

duration: 0:40
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.learn.show

role: spoken

This is a map of families, not a catalog. Fukami and Taira, in 2023, compress the flow past a wing at Reynolds number 100 into three latent variables. Principal components miss the vortex those three variables keep. Bousquet, Nore, and Lucor, in 2025, then compare autoencoders on Navier-Stokes fields, including a von Karman flow. Variational models keep the latent symmetries better. More regularization makes the reconstruction worse. Learn the family, not one paper by its name.

## g12.gen

duration: 0:45
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.gen.show

role: spoken

One family learns a path from noise to the data. Flow matching is that family, and FunDiff is the field reference the offer names. Another family trains on an equation residual scored sample by sample. PIDM is that family. A third family forces zero divergence by projection. PCDM puts a Leray step on a three-dimensional velocity. Genuist compared several strategies on turbulence, near the data and far from it. No strategy wins everywhere. Strict constraints behave better near the data and over a short time. Soft constraints can behave better far away and over a long rollout.

## g12.lawlit

duration: 0:35
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.lawlit.show

role: spoken

A stationary regime constrains the cloud of frames, not one frame. Those identities are the first family here. The Weak Generative Sampler learns an invariant law up to dimension 100. Tilting is the second family. It reweights a law you already have, and it does not draw a new field. The third family scores a weak residual on each sample. A score on one sample is not an identity of the law. Keep the three families apart.

## g12.gaps

duration: 0:40
badge: CONJECTURE

### cue.g12.gaps.show

role: spoken

Six gaps shape the internship. No generative model of a dynamo was found. No joint hard constraint covers both divergences in three dimensions. No cited generator treats a walled domain. None is scored on helicity, an energy budget, or topology. None conditions on both Reynolds numbers and crosses a threshold. No coupled comparison adds a distributional constraint on a field.

### cue.g12.gaps.caveat

role: spoken

The search closed on November 6, 2026. The absence is medium confidence, because an absence cannot be proved. E1, E2, and E3 do not close these gaps on a bounded three-dimensional domain. That claim is high confidence.
