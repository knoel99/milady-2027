# g12 — The map of the field

## g12.learn

duration: 0:40
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.learn.show

role: spoken

This is a map of families, not a catalog. Fukami and Taira, in 2023, compress the flow past a wing at Reynolds number 100 into three latent variables. A latent variable is a compressed representation of the field. Principal components miss the vortex those three variables keep. Bousquet, Nore, and Lucor, in 2025, then compare autoencoders on Navier-Stokes fields, including a von Karman flow. Variational models keep the latent symmetries better. More regularization makes the reconstruction worse. Learn the family, not one paper by its name.

## g12.gen

duration: 0:45
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.gen.show

role: spoken
screen: FunDiff, PIDM, PCDM

One family learns a path from noise to the data. Flow matching is that family. Another family trains on an equation residual scored sample by sample. A third family forces zero divergence by a correction. A Leray projection is that correction. It removes the part of the velocity that would leave a divergence. Genuist compared several strategies on turbulence, near the data and far from it. No strategy wins everywhere. Strict constraints behave better near the data and over a short time. Soft constraints can behave better far away and over a long rollout.

## g12.lawlit

duration: 0:35
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g12.lawlit.show

role: spoken

A stationary regime constrains the cloud of frames, not one frame. Those identities are the first family here. The Weak Generative Sampler learns an invariant law up to dimension 100. Tilting is the second family. Tilting changes the weight of the samples. It changes the probabilities under an existing law. The third family scores a weak residual on each sample. A score on one sample is not an identity of the law. Keep the three families apart.

## g12.gaps

duration: 0:46
badge: CONJECTURE

### cue.g12.gaps.show

role: spoken

Six gaps shape the MHD works that were examined. No generative model of a dynamo was found there. No joint hard constraint covers both divergences in three dimensions. None of those works treats a walled domain. None is scored on helicity, on magnetic energy budgets, or on topology. None conditions on both Reynolds numbers and crosses a threshold. No coupled comparison adds a distributional constraint on a field.

### cue.g12.gaps.caveat

role: spoken

The search closed on November 6, 2026. The absence is medium confidence, because an absence cannot be proved. E1, E2, and E3 do not close these gaps on a bounded three-dimensional domain. That claim is high confidence.
