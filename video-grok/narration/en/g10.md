# g10 — Where the constraint is true

## g10.classes

duration: 0:45
badge: PROVED

### cue.g10.classes.show

role: spoken

A constraint can sit in four places. C1 is a linear local constraint on each field. C2 is a nonlinear local constraint on each field. C3 is a symmetry of the law. C4 is a stationarity identity of the law. A symmetry has to survive every parameter, even one never used in training. Linear means a straight condition on the field itself. Nonlinear means the condition can bend. Neither class is a penalty stuck on after the fact. A short bar is not a prize. The place of the constraint is the claim. The later penalty is not one of these four classes.

## g10.sym

duration: 0:30
badge: PROVED

### cue.g10.sym.show

role: spoken

Changing the sign of the magnetic field B is a symmetry of the law. Equivariance holds that symmetry at every parameter, including parameters with no data. Budget identities do not set the weights of the two polarities. A budget can balance and still put the wrong weight on each side. The identities can be true and still leave the weights free. A balanced budget does not choose how much weight sits on each polarity.

## g10.bias

duration: 0:25
badge: PROVED

### cue.g10.bias.show

role: spoken

The square of an empirical mean over a finite batch penalizes variance. The product of two independent half-batch means removes that bias. One squared batch mean charges the cloud for being wide, even when the true mean is already right. The identity can clean a loss. It does not by itself pick a model.
