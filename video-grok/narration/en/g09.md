# g09 — Drawing a field from noise

## g09.path

duration: 0:40
badge: PROVED

### cue.g09.path.show

role: spoken

Start from Gaussian noise. Segments point toward the data. The generative velocity is the conditional mean of those directions, at a fixed position, a fixed flow time, and a fixed parameter. Each segment is one step from noise toward a snapshot. The drawing is a toy. It is not a measurement. The arrows are not a fluid velocity, and they are not a magnetic field. Flow time is not physical time. A step of flow time is not a step of the fluid.

## g09.cond

duration: 0:35
badge: PROVED

### cue.g09.cond.show

role: spoken

The same network receives the parameter. The parameter enters beside the noise, so one network serves the whole sweep. A filled tick is a value the training saw. An open tick is a test. The network has to answer on an open tick without having stored that value. Filled and open ticks show that test. Open ticks here are interpolation. They do not step past the trained range. That is the same kind of test you meet again when a later bench is bounded.

## g09.why

duration: 0:40
badge: PROVED

### cue.g09.why.show

role: spoken

The path is straight so a stationarity loss can differentiate through the flow. If the loss cares about a stationary cloud, its gradient has to travel back along the path that built the sample. A straight path keeps that trip simple. The scheme that integrates the flow is called Heun. Heun is the stepper. Diffusion stays open. The offer left both samplers available, and these runs picked the straight path. Flow time is still not physical time. It is the coordinate of the walk, not the time in the fluid equations.

## g09.sizes

duration: 0:25
badge: MEASURED

### cue.g09.sizes.show

role: spoken

Variants A, B, and C of E1 train for 12,000 iterations on an MLP of width 256. E2 trains for 6,000 iterations at width 1024. One sample is generated in 50 Heun steps. Those training iterations are not the Heun steps. Width is the hidden layer. An iteration updates the training. A Heun step builds one sample.
