Is how often a date, or a frequency?

### cue.g01.rate.show

role: spoken

A law is a probability distribution over states. A state is one configuration of the field. The law gives each state a weight. A frequency is the total weight of one kind of state. One kind is one polarity. The other kind is the opposite polarity. How often means that frequency. It does not mean a year. A date would place one reversal on a timeline. A frequency does not name the year. You can know how common a polarity is and still not know when the field flips. The weight and the calendar are different questions.

### cue.g01.rate.caveat

role: spoken

A model can have a mean waiting time between those states. A waiting time is how long one state lasts before the field changes. The frequency does not state that duration. The mean wait belongs to the model. It is not an Earth measurement. No Earth rate is stated.
