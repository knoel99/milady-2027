## g01.rate

badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g01.rate.predict

role: spoken
pause: 3s

Is how often a date, or a reversal rate?

### cue.g01.rate.show

role: spoken

A law is a probability distribution over states. The law tells us how probable each state is. A state is one configuration of the field. The reversal rate tells us how often the field switches polarity per unit time. Those are different quantities. Knowing how probable a polarity is does not say how often the field switches. Together with the evolution equation, the state distribution lets us estimate how often polarity changes. A date would place one reversal on a timeline. The rate does not name the year.

### cue.g01.rate.caveat

role: spoken

A mean waiting time is how long one polarity lasts before the field switches. It is not the reversal rate. The reversal rate counts switches per unit time, and that count needs the evolution equation together with the state distribution. The mean wait belongs to the model. It is not an Earth measurement. No Earth rate is stated.
