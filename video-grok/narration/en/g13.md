## g13.bets

duration: 1:18
badge: CONJECTURE

### cue.g13.bets.show

role: spoken

Nothing has been run. The target is both relative divergences below 10 to the minus 12. It belongs to a periodic decoder built from potentials, read by discrete operators that match that representation. Coulomb gauge alone does not guarantee that target. If the divergences come out larger, run a numerical check before calling the miss new physics.

### cue.g13.bets.blur

role: spoken

Errors should be larger near the three ABC thresholds than far away. A smooth generator is assumed to miss the jump. Confidence is high on that miss, and only medium on a finer grid, because the second threshold is already blurred.

### cue.g13.bets.tg

role: spoken

A dynamo should appear, and the threshold should move with the magnetic Prandtl number. At 64 cubed, Reynolds number 100 stays provisional until the spectra decay before the cutoff. Confidence is low while the forcing convention is not yet the one in the paper. No Taylor-Green threshold is stated.

### cue.g13.bets.anchor

role: spoken

Anchoring limits how far fine-tuning can move from the starting model. Without the anchor, one seed can diverge, as in E1. Confidence is medium with anchoring and low without it. Do not say the loss works in three dimensions.
