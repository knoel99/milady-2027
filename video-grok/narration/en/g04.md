## g04.cowling

### cue.g04.cowling.show

A magnetic field that is too symmetric does not sustain itself. Cowling in 1933, through Tobias, says an axisymmetric field that vanishes at infinity cannot be maintained by dynamo action. The poloidal part leaves the body and returns. The toroidal part circles inside. The poloidal part decays, and then the toroidal part has nothing left to feed it.

## g04.reduced

duration: 0:30
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g04.reduced.show

role: spoken

Pétrélis and Fauve give a normal form that couples a dipole and a quadrupole. Gissinger writes a deterministic model with three modes. A later reduction called DQVU is fit on 2000 snapshots from a VKS simulation. Those snapshots are not public. The three-mode model and the four-mode fit are not the same object. One is a deterministic toy with a known crisis. The other is a fit to snapshots the sources do not hold.
