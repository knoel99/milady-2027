# g04 — A century of dynamo hunting

## g04.cowling

duration: 0:25
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g04.cowling.show

role: spoken

A magnetic field that is too symmetric does not sustain itself. Cowling in 1933, through the statement carried by Tobias, says an axisymmetric field that vanishes at infinity cannot be maintained by dynamo action. The poloidal part decays, and then the toroidal part has nothing left to feed it.

duration: 0:35

### cue.g04.sim.show

role: spoken

Nore and colleagues in 2018 find that the threshold moves from 90 plus or minus 5 to 130 plus or minus 10 when the boundary condition changes from pseudo-vacuum to vacuum. Those values sit at a kinetic Reynolds number of 1.5 times 10 cubed and a relative permeability of 50. The LATU grid has 384 cubed points, and the impellers are put in by penalization.

## g04.reduced

duration: 0:30
badge: CONTEXT

### cue.g04.reduced.show

role: spoken

Pétrélis and Fauve give a normal form that couples a dipole and a quadrupole. Gissinger writes a deterministic model with three modes. A later reduction called DQVU is fit on 2000 snapshots from a VKS simulation. Those snapshots are not public. The three-mode model and the four-mode fit are not the same object. One is a deterministic toy with a known crisis. The other is a fit to snapshots the sources do not hold.
