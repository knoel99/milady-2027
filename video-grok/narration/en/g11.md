Projection one sample at a time moves the cloud the wrong way. A tiny spread on each frame is not a better cloud. The loss on short C lets one seed diverge. A loss can also throw a run away.

## g11.floor

duration: 0:30
badge: MEASURED

### cue.g11.floor.show

role: spoken

A metric with no floor says nothing. Zero is a tempting baseline, and it is the wrong one. Sampling noise sits above zero. Draw the floor, then read the gap. A bar that does not clear the floor is compatible with noise. Cross-helicity is the twist between velocity and magnetic field. The cross-helicity residue does not rank these models. A smaller residue there is not a better law. Leave it off the ranking.

## g11.e3

duration: 1:20
badge: MEASURED

### cue.g11.e3.show

role: spoken

E3 checks thresholds of a solver. The flow is forced, and it is called ABC. There is no generator. Rm is 1 over eta, and eta is magnetic diffusivity, not a viscosity. A larger Rm is a weaker diffusivity, not a new fluid. The report rounds the thresholds to 8.87, 17.6, and 26.8. The published windows are 8.9, 17.5, and above 27.

### cue.g11.e3.caveat

role: spoken

17.6 is the interpolation of the sign of the magnetic growth rate, lambda, on the second half of the run. Lambda is that growth rate, not a forcing and not a tilt. Shift the window and the sign at 17.5 can flip. The second threshold is known to about 0.1 of 17.5. The third is known to about one unit. The third is not a sharp disagreement with the window above 27. The report only claims about one unit there. These are not confidence intervals. Do not draw them as error bars. At Rm 17.5 the sign changes with the window.
