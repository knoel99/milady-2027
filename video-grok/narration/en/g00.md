### cue.g00.needle.show

role: spoken

This picture is an illustration, not to time scale. It is not a geological clock, and it is not a sodium-lab clock. A compass needle lines up with the magnetic field. When that field turns over, the needle points south. The swing shows the change of direction. It does not show how long the change took. We do not say when the needle turns. This opening states no rate.
