## g05.project

duration: 0:30

### cue.g05.project.show

role: spoken

The project is MilaDy, grant ANR-25-CE51-0077. It starts in January 2026 and runs for 48 months. The ANR funding is 560,612 euros. Caroline Nore coordinates it. There are two objectives. The first studies reversals and builds reduced models that can name a precursor, a reversal, and a recovery. The second explores DRESDYN through the ratio of precession frequency to rotation frequency.

The 2027 offer asks for fields conditioned on the control parameters. Trajectories are not the first object. pause: 2s

Before the split, ask whether one picture can hold a time derivative.

### cue.g05.snapshot.show

role: spoken

A snapshot of an unsteady equation does not contain the time derivative. Every state in that picture is an initial state of some solution. You cannot compute the residual of the evolution from the picture alone. What you can require of one picture is a constraint with no time in it. Zero divergence is that kind of constraint. The mean energy budget is different. It is a constraint on the distribution. An identity computed on one frame is not that constraint. Keep the two apart.
